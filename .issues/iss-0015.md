# ISS-0015 — Person calendar and reminders panel

GitHub: [#15 — Person calendar and reminders panel](https://github.com/humanitas-labs/rx/issues/15)

A calendar control on the open conversation (top right of the reader) opens a right-hand panel of upcoming events — and reminders — that include that person.

Scope sketch:

- Resolve the conversation's participant(s) to EventKit people the same way Contacts resolves handles to names.
- Show upcoming calendar events shared with them, plus reminders that mention or are assigned to them.
- Read-only in v0: no creating or editing events from rx.
- 1:1 conversations first. Groups are unresolved until a person-picker exists.

This is a new product surface, not a Messages-reader gap. EventKit permission is a new TCC grant and needs an onboarding row when it ships.

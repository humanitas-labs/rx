# ISS-0018 — Delivered and Read receipts under the last outbound bubble

GitHub: [#18 — Delivered and Read receipts under the last outbound bubble](https://github.com/humanitas-labs/rx/issues/18)

The Figma reader shows a `Delivered` label under the latest outgoing bubble. The inventory marked that implemented; the thread does not render it. Messages.app then replaces `Delivered` with `Read` (and a time) once the other side opens the message.

Wanted:

- Under the latest *outbound* bubble only: `Delivered`, then `Read` when the source says so. Earlier outbound messages stay unlabeled, like Messages.
- Drive both states from Apple's message row (`date_delivered` / `date_read` or the live equivalent — confirm on the current schema). Do not invent a second receipt store in `workflow.db`.
- When the label first appears or changes Delivered → Read, play the short Messages-style slide/fade in. No bounce loop.
- Pending / failed send stay on the composer (already there). This issue is the source receipt under a verified bubble.

Not in scope: sending a read receipt or clearing Messages.app's unread badge when Andrew opens a thread in rx. That remains a supported-surface gap (spec §3.4, privacy.md). We *display* their read of our mail; we do not signal that we read theirs.

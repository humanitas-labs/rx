# ISS-0010 — Play and expand photos and videos in the reader

GitHub: [#10 — Play and expand photos and videos in the reader](https://github.com/humanitas-labs/rx/issues/10)

Images already render inline. Videos do not play, and neither photos nor videos can be opened larger.

Wanted:

- Tap/click a photo or video to expand it (lightbox / full-size overlay). Escape and click-out dismiss.
- Videos play in place and in the expanded view (local attachment bytes over the existing read-only attachment protocol).
- Unsupported or missing local files stay on the current file-chip fallback.

No copy of attachment bytes into rx storage. Same path scoping as today's image render (`~/Library/Messages` only).

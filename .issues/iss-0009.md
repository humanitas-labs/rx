# ISS-0009 — Person calendar panel: upcoming events and reminders

GitHub: [#9 — Person calendar panel: upcoming events and reminders](https://github.com/humanitas-labs/rx/issues/9)

## What

Open a conversation with someone and see the calendar around them. A small calendar glyph in the reader header (top-right) opens a right panel of upcoming events that include that person, plus any reminders attached to those events.

## Why

Messages without time-context are incomplete. If we have lunch Tuesday or a reminder to send the deck, that should sit next to the thread, not in a separate Calendar window.

## Design

Frame: [RX / Person calendar](https://www.figma.com/design/zL4rZa8PPB6rj0KBNiunE7/RX?node-id=100-2703)

## Behavior (v0 sketch)

- Afford the calendar from the reader header, 1:1 conversations first. Groups can wait.
- Match events by participant email / phone against EventKit attendees (same Contacts resolution as names).
- Panel lists upcoming events soonest-first, with title, time, and location when present.
- Reminders that belong to the event (or are explicitly linked to the person) appear under or beside it.
- Empty state: no upcoming events. Denied Calendar / Reminders permission is a row in the panel, not a hard block on the inbox.
- Local only. EventKit read. Do not write events or store calendar payloads in `workflow.db`.

## Out of scope here

- Creating or editing events from rx
- Cross-Mac calendar sync of rx state
- Showing the full personal calendar, unrelated to the open conversation
